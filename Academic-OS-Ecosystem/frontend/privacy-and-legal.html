1. Explicit & Implicit Behavioral Data Scopes

The MSc Data Science and AI Progress Tracker monitors operational actions to establish study pattern metrics and generate academic performance analytics. Data collection is categorized as follows:

  • Explicit Progress Assets: Student study states, syllabus module interactions, checkpoint accomplishments, assignment metrics, and quiz values.
  • Implicit Behavioral Metrics: User dwell time, navigation interactions, session logging, clickstream trajectories, active vs. idle state durations, study session timestamps, and device identifiers.
  • Cognitive Tasks & Metadata: Personal notes, flashcards, study reminders, and scheduling delay responses.
Encryption In-Transit and At-Rest
All data logged via the BITS Student Terminal is protected under absolute HTTPS (TLS 1.3) protocols. Data residing inside the Cloud synchronization database utilizes AES-256 standards.

2. Predictive Modeling & Machine Learning

To provide personal performance projections and interventions, telemetry streams are evaluated through dedicated machine learning architectures. Your behavioral features feed the following pipelines:

XGBoost Score Projections

Forecasts final score values based on current assignment, quiz metrics, and rolling study consistency averages.

Gaussian Mixture Clustering

Identifies peak productivity brackets (e.g. Morning, Evening) to auto-calibrate optimal notification intervals.

LightGBM Dropout Classifiers

Categorizes study risk levels into Low, Medium, or High based on streak loss and missed milestones.

Sequence Modeling (LSTM)

Evaluates past weeks' actions to project upcoming module completion percentages.

These algorithms continuously adapt. Personal profiles are compiled exclusively for private diagnostic purposes to foster academic achievement.

3. Vector Storage & RAG Processing

When using the Integrated Academic Assistant, academic assets and student notes are dynamically parsed and processed:

  • Content Vectorization: Notes, quiz questions, and textbooks are mapped into a dense vector space using secure embedding algorithms.
  • Vector Storage: Generated vectors are stored in secure indexed databases (such as Qdrant or Pinecone) linked to your unique BITS Student terminal ID.
  • Generative LLM Pipelines: Contextual queries are handled through secure API frameworks (OpenAI / Gemini). Content passed to the model is scrubbed of all institutional student directory listings. No data is used for external training models.

4. Institutional Disclosures

Data stored in this tracker is intended primarily for the student's self-improvement. However, to guarantee professional academic growth, academic progression dashboards may be shared under strict parameters:

  1. Dean of Academics & Course Instructors: To monitor overall engagement and evaluate structural design effectiveness.
  2. System Administrators: To guarantee system performance, manage user terminal sessions, and maintain data storage security.

5. Student Rights

As a student of BITS Pilani, you maintain distinct privacy protections over your terminal analytical records:

  • Right to Inspection: You can view all metrics compiled by your terminal directly through the interface.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can export logged analytical parameters into structured CSV/JSON payloads.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request immediate erasure of your historical analytical values, resetting your learning weights in the database.

6. Data Retention Policy

Academic telemetry metrics, notes embeddings, and completion logs are stored for the active duration of the MSc program. Upon graduation, program completion, or student profile termination, all associated personal identifier mappings are permanently anonymized or deleted within 60 academic calendar days.

1. Academic Domain Authorization

Access to the Student Terminal, synchronization utilities, and performance modeling algorithms is strictly restricted to students currently enrolled in the BITS Pilani MSc Data Science and AI program. Users must authenticate exclusively via their official @bitspilani-digital.edu.in email domain.

Unauthorized logins, credential sharing, or accessing the application with non-institutional email domains will result in instant session termination and a referral to academic authorities.

2. Academic Integrity Rules

The academic tracking systems, dashboard summaries, and AI recommendations are designed to foster study consistency. Students agree to follow these guidelines:

  • Accurate Progress Verification: Users must not simulate or falsify progress metrics by automating module checkmarks without reviewing the coursework content.
  • Legitimate AI Assistant Usage: The LLM Assistant is an educational aid. Generating direct, unauthored responses for graded quizzes or assignments is a violation of the BITS Pilani Honor Code.

3. Predictive Logic Limitations

The Machine Learning analytical modules (expected performance models, dropout risks, and grade projections) are calculated models based on study patterns. These values are designed for guidance and do not represent guaranteed grades. Actual trimester outcomes are governed solely by course coordinators, exam performance, and standard grading policies.

4. Academic RAG Assistant SLA

While BITS Pilani strives for high-quality accuracy in model retrieval operations, the Academic Assistant may occasionally serve contextually inaccurate responses. Students are expected to verify all academic facts, mathematical calculations, and programming syntax against official BITS course resources and prescribed textbooks.

5. Terminal Security & Usage Integrity

Users must not compromise the integrity of the tracking application. The following actions are strictly prohibited:

  • Reverse-engineering the tracking API endpoints or scraping structural database schemas.
  • Automating high-volume database queries that degrade the system for other students.
  • Injecting malicious content, script modules, or broken payloads into personal notes stored in the database.

6. Academic Breach Recourse

Failure to comply with these terms, domain rules, or academic integrity policies may result in immediate suspension of tracker synchronization privileges, deletion of analytical profiles, and academic disciplinary action under the regulatory framework of the Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani.
